## Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Low Task Quality
- **Mitigation**: Start with dry-run mode; manual review before execution
- **Fallback**: Use heuristic generator instead of LLM

### Risk 2: SLO Violations
- **Mitigation**: Auto-rollback on critical violations; 30-min cooldown
- **Fallback**: Pause dogfooding; investigate root cause

### Risk 3: Insufficient Health Score Improvement
- **Mitigation**: Focus on high-impact tasks (coverage, debt reduction)
- **Fallback**: Extend dogfooding period to 3 weeks

### Risk 4: Too Many Bugs Discovered
- **Mitigation**: Prioritize critical bugs; defer low-priority to M4
- **Fallback**: Extend analysis phase to 1 week
